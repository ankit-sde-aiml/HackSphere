GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How can I enroll in a program?

Open a published program, sign in with Google and follow the available payment instructions. Access begins once enrollment is approved after payment verification.

Can I enroll in more than one program?

Yes. Each approved enrollment has its own classroom, calendar, assignments and evaluations.

When do lessons become available?

Lessons and materials are available when your assigned Trainer or Guide publishes them and their scheduled date arrives.

Is a placement or job guaranteed?

No. HackSphere provides technical learning and project-based experience, not guaranteed placements, jobs, stipends or accreditation.
