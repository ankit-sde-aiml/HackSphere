KNOW BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Frequently asked questions

How are daily lessons unlocked?+

Every lesson has a scheduled date. Activities become available on that date; scores and unfinished assignments do not block tomorrow's schedule.

How can I join live classes?+

Enrolled students see Join Live Class when the scheduled session is available. Access is checked on HackSphere; Google Meet still controls final admission.

When is my internship guide assigned?+

Admin allocates an Internship Guide when the internship phase starts. The guide manages project reviews, scoring and completion recommendations.

Can I join multiple programs?+

Yes. Each enrollment maintains its own timeline, assignments, training score, internship progress and documents.
