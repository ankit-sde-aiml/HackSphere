## Bugs found and corrected

1. **Quiz scoring SQL alias collision** caused submitted attempts to fail; fixed by rebuilding `submit_quiz`. Real Student-role submission now returns 100% on QA sample.
2. **No designated Guide on program creation**; added `program_guides` and atomic `create_program_with_team` RPC, while keeping per-student Guide allocation date-gated.
3. **CSV importing duplicate rows**; added server-side validation and duplicate-skip behavior.
4. **Unsafe claim that a test UPI receipt had been approved**; avoided by creating explicit QA enrollment with no real payment and disabling public document verification.
5. **Google Meet links previously absent from schedule**; added restricted meeting registration and time-window join APIs, plus Trainer OAuth consent and batch-sync interface. Still untested against Google Meet provider.
6. **Course team/live host attribution when Admin imports CSV**; importer now uses the program's designated Trainer/Guide instead of automatically attributing the session to Admin.
7. **Student/admin navigation clutter and mobile typography**; grouped role navigation, isolated program create/edit/detail pages, separate payment history and policies, larger field typography and expanded footer.
8. **No transparent merchant policy navigation**; added public About, Pricing, Contact, Terms, Privacy, Refund and Digital Delivery pages with an Admin-editable business profile.
9. **QA cohorts mixed with public paid programs**; QA cohort is hidden from public catalog and visibly labelled for authorized testers.
10. **Quiz answers exposed if stored in public question schema**; secured answer keys in `app_private` and server-grades via RPC.

## Google Meet integration: remaining external setup

Google Meet REST API creates real meeting spaces via `POST https://meet.googleapis.com/v2/spaces`, requiring the OAuth scope:
`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/meetings.space.created`.

Completed in source:
- Worker verifies Supabase JWT and Google OAuth token belong to the same Google identity.
- Only designated staff/Admin can create a meeting, with `RESTRICTED` Meet access.
- Teacher may grant Google consent, and HackSphere batches pending scheduled sessions into Meet spaces.
- Students receive a link only when actively enrolled and near the scheduled class time.

**Not completed:** Google Meet API enabling/consent by the Trainer, production API success test, recording download integration, provider refresh-token storage, and unattended background creation without Trainer action. Pending sessions remain marked as pending; no fake Meet URL exists.

External action: In Google Cloud enable **Google Meet API**, ensure the Trainer Google account is in the OAuth testing audience, authorize the sensitive Meet scope, and use the Trainer Dashboard **Connect Google Meet & generate links** control. Production verification of Google's sensitive OAuth scope may be required.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/workspace/meet/api/reference/rest/v2/spaces/create

## Trainer/Guide LMS workflow

- Trainers can schedule/date training activities, upload recordings and PDF notes to a protected bucket, create quiz metadata/questions with private answer keys, review submitted work, and enter training evaluations.
- Guides are designated on programs at creation; they can schedule internship lessons and prepare materials. Actual **student-to-Guide allocations still require the internship phase start** and Admin action.
- Guide internship project scoring is restricted to allocated students. The current live QA cohort is not yet in internship phase, so a genuine Guide review is intentionally not simulated.
- Recording file upload is subject to Supabase storage quotas; a real uploaded file and playback must be tested with signed-in roles.

## Razorpay review readiness

Public review pages and route navigation have been created, but **merchant onboarding is NOT ready** until truthful business information is supplied and approved.

Required owner inputs:
- Legal trading name and business type
- Public support email and support phone
- Physical business/contact address
- Real refund and cancellation eligibility, request steps and processing timeline
- Actual published program fee pages and customer service availability
- Sample invoice, payment test account/test access as requested by the gateway, and matching KYC/bank documents

The Admin Settings form publishes only approved merchant fields. **Do not fabricate merchant contact details, guaranteed refunds, credentials or accreditation.** Live Razorpay keys, webhook signature verification, idempotency, settlement reconciliation and production gateway activation have **not been integrated**. Existing manual UPI workflow remains separate.

Razorpay review guidance: https://github.com/razorpay/markdown-docs/blob/master/payments/dashboard/account-settings/business-website-details.md
